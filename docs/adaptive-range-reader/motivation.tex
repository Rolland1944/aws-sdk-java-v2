\section{Background and Motivation}
\label{sec:moti}
In this section, we discuss the I/O model that RECAS targets and the observations that motivate our design.  
%This section sets up the I/O model that RECAS targets and the observations that motivate its design. The primary goal is to reduce the number of remote Range GETs; a secondary constraint is to preserve effective concurrency, so that fewer requests are not bought by starving the reader of parallel scan units. Together, these goals call for online caching and request shaping on the read path, and for feedback-driven adjustment of the write-time layout.
